Moderation

Mutes, bans & warnings

Silence players with a mute or a soft mute, let warning points punish repeat offenders automatically, and lock or slow down the chat.

These are the chat moderation tools. A mute stops a player from talking. A soft mute lets them keep typing, but nobody else sees their messages. Warning points add up every time a player breaks a chat rule, and punish them automatically when they reach a limit.

Mutes, warning points and the chat controls work out of the box. Bans, kicks and freezes are part of the optional staff suite.

#Mute a player

text
/mute <player> [time] [reason]
/unmute <player>

Time is optional: 10m, 2h, 1d, 1w. Without a time the mute is permanent. Mutes survive restarts. Both commands need chatalyst.command.mute (operators have it).

A muted player cannot chat. By default they also cannot send mail, write on signs or books, rename items on an anvil, or use a few chat commands. You choose all of that in moderation.yml:

yaml
Mute:
  Block_Mail: true       # muted players cannot send /inbox mail
  Block_Signs: true      # muted players cannot place text on signs
  Block_Books: true      # muted players cannot write/sign books
  Block_Anvil: true      # muted players cannot give items a NEW name on an anvil (repairs still work)
  Blocked_Commands: [me, say, msg, tell, w, whisper, m, r, reply, mail]

/helpop is left out on purpose: a muted player can still reach the staff.

#Soft mute

Add -s to mute a player silently:

text
/mute -s <player> [time] [reason]

The player can keep typing and sees their own messages as normal, but nobody else receives them. It is useful for a suspected spammer or troll who would just make a new account after a normal mute. A soft mute only hides public chat; the Mute options above do not apply to it.

#Warning points

Warning points are on by default. Each rule with Points in rules.yml (and the swear filter) adds points when a player breaks it. When the total reaches a threshold, Chatalyst runs the commands you list. Points reset after a while without new infractions.

yaml
Warning_Points:
  Enabled: true
  # After this time without new infractions, the player's points reset.
  # Formats: 10m, 2h, 1d, 1w, or "perm" to never decay.
  Decay_After: 1d
  # Points added by a manual /warn from staff (0 = warns notify and record, but add none).
  Manual_Warn_Points: 1
  Thresholds:
    3:
      - "mute {player} 10m Too many chat infractions"
    5:
      - "kick {player} Repeated chat infractions"

{player} and {points} are replaced in the commands. Check or clear a player's points with /warnings <player> [clear] (chatalyst.command.warnings).

#Automatic mute for spam

The anti-spam gives spammers warnings and then mutes them for longer each time: 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, and so on. It is set under Anti_Spam.Auto_Mute in moderation.yml. See Filter, anti-spam & anti-bot.

#Control the whole chat

Command What it does Permission
/clearchat Pushes everyone's chat out of view. Staff with chatalyst.command.clearchat.bypass keep theirs. chatalyst.command.clearchat
/chatlock Locks the public chat. /chatunlock opens it again. chatalyst.command.chatlock
/slowmode <seconds|off> Forces a wait between messages for everyone. chatalyst.command.slowmode

#Bans, kicks and freezes

/ban, /tempban, /kick, /warn, /freeze and the rest come with the staff suite, which ships off so it never fights a staff plugin you already use.
