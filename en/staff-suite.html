Moderation

Staff suite

An optional, menu-driven moderation toolkit with bans, kicks, freezes, connection bans, vanish, staff mode, history and inventory checks.

The staff suite gives your team everything to handle a problem player from one menu: ban, kick, freeze, vanish, check inventories and read a player's history. Staff do not need to memorize commands; /staff opens a dashboard with buttons for each action.

It ships off, so Chatalyst never fights a staff plugin you already use. Turn it on only if you want Chatalyst to be your moderation plugin.

#Turn it on

In moderation.yml:

yaml
Staff_Moderation:
  Enabled: true

Then restart the server or run /cht reload. While it is off, none of its commands are registered.

Note

The record of player connections is kept even while the suite is off. Read Connections, alts and privacy.

#What it includes

Tool Commands
Dashboard /staff opens the menu: player list, reports, frozen players and tools.
Punishments /kick, /warn, /ban, /tempban, /unban, /unwarn. Reasons come from presets or are typed privately.
Connection bans and mutes /banip, /unbanip, /ipmute, /unipmute: act on the account and its connection.
Freeze /freeze, /unfreeze: stop a player for a review. Leaving during the review gives a permanent ban.
Vanish and staff mode /vanish: invisible, silent and unable to change the world while watching. /staff on: hidden from tab, /msg and /tpa, read-only chests, and back where you started.
X-ray /xray: see the chosen ores around you through stone. Optional detector of suspicious mining.
Inventories and chests /invsee, /echest, /vaultsee (needs PlayerVaultsX), /clearinventory, /chestview (open any container read-only).
Player lookup /check, /seen, /history, /note.
Accounts /dupeip, /ipreport, /iphistory, /namehistory, /lastuuid.
Active punishments /checkban, /checkmute, /checkwarn, /banlist, /mutelist, /warnlist.
Team control /staffhistory, /staffrollback, /prunehistory, /lockdown, /banstyle.
Teleport /tpto, /tphere.

Most tools also have Spanish aliases. The full list with permissions is in Commands. chatalyst.staff.* gives almost everything; give chatalyst.command.lockdown, chatalyst.command.staffrollback, chatalyst.command.prunehistory, chatalyst.command.banstyle and chatalyst.staff.ip separately, to high ranks only.

Staff can also review players and sanctions from a browser with the web panel.

#Who owns /ban, /kick and /vanish

With the suite on, Chatalyst takes the plain command names even if another plugin registered them first, so its tools are the ones that answer. If you turn the suite off again, the other plugin gets them back.

yaml
  Command_Priority: true
  Exempt_Ops: true
  • Command_Priority: false only takes names no other plugin uses. The /chatalyst:ban form always works.
  • Exempt_Ops: true means operators can never be punished or frozen. Set it to false on small servers where trusted players keep OP.

#Punishment announcements

Bans, kicks, warnings and unbans are announced in chat to everyone, on the whole network. Add -s to keep one quiet:

text
/ban -s Steve X-Ray

A silent punishment is only shown to staff with chatalyst.notify.silent. For /mute, -s means something else: a soft mute.

/banstyle opens a menu to pick how bans look: one of four chat designs, a sound, and an animated title on screen. It saves the choice to moderation.yml under Staff_Moderation.Broadcast. Set Staff_Moderation.Broadcast.Enabled: false if only staff should see punishments.

#The ban screen and appeals

A banned player sees the reason, who banned them, the time left, the date and the punishment number. Add your appeal link and it shows on that screen too:

yaml
Staff_Moderation:
  Appeal_Link: "https://example.com/appeal"
  Appeal_Link_es: "https://example.com/es/apelar"

Empty (the default) shows no link. Appeal_Link_es is used for players with the game in Spanish. Write only the address or plain text: colors are removed.

#Repeat offenders

Several kicks or warning points turn into an automatic ban, and each new one lasts longer.

yaml
  Escalation:
    Enabled: true
    Kicks_For_Ban: 3
    Kick_Window: 30d
    Kick_Merge_Minutes: 10
    Count_Console_Kicks: false
    Warn_Points_For_Ban: 8
    Ban_Durations: [7d, 30d, perm]
  • Three kicks within 30 days ban the player. Kicks by the same staff member less than 10 minutes apart count as one.
  • Reaching 8 warning points also bans them. 0 turns either trigger off.
  • The first automatic ban lasts 7 days, the second 30 days, and then it is permanent.
  • Console kicks (for example from an anticheat) do not count unless Count_Console_Kicks: true.

Warning

Warn_Points_For_Ban shares its points with Warning_Points. Keep it higher than your highest threshold there. With the same number, the ban wins and that last step never runs. The default thresholds end at 8, so raise Warn_Points_For_Ban if you want the 6 hour mute to happen.

#Staff mode

/staff on (or the mode button in /staff) swaps your inventory for the staff kit. Your real inventory is saved to disk first and comes back when you leave, even after a crash.

While you are in staff mode:

  • Nobody knows you are online. You leave the tab list and the player counter, and nobody can /msg you, /tpa to you or tab-complete your name. This covers Chatalyst's own commands and plugins that respect hidden players (tested with EssentialsX). It applies to everyone entering staff mode, with or without the /vanish permission.
  • You can check any chest. Right-click a chest, barrel, shulker or any container to see a read-only copy: no lid, no sound, nothing can be taken out. Each look goes to the staff audit.
  • You come back where you started. Teleport to whoever you need to watch; when you leave staff mode you are sent back to where you turned it on.

The kit slots (counted from 0): inspector (0), freeze (1), X-ray vision (2), staff panel (4), go to a player (6), vanish (7) and leave (8). A tool only appears if you have its permission.

yaml
  Staff_Mode:
    Vanish_On_Enter: true
    Allow_Flight: true
    Night_Vision: true
    Noclip: false
    Decoy: false
    AntiXray_Bypass: false
    Chest_View: true
    Return_On_Exit: true
    Toggle_On_Command: false
Option What it does
Night_Vision Night vision while in staff mode.
Noclip Flying into a wall switches you to spectator only while you are inside blocks.
Decoy Leaves a mannequin with your skin and armor where you turned the mode on (Minecraft 1.21.9 or newer).
AntiXray_Bypass Shows real ores instead of Paper's fake ones. Needs anticheat.anti-xray.use-permission: true in config/paper-world-defaults.yml.
Toggle_On_Command true = a plain /staff turns the mode on and off. The dashboard stays on /staff menu.

#Rank hierarchy

Nobody can freeze, ban, kick, mute, warn, IP-ban, edit the inventory of or clear the inventory of a staff member of the same rank or higher. A helper cannot freeze another helper; a moderator can. Ordinary players are not affected.

A rank is a number. Give it with the permission chatalyst.rank.<number> (for example helper chatalyst.rank.10, moderator chatalyst.rank.20, admin chatalyst.rank.30), or let Chatalyst read the weight of your LuckPerms groups. The highest wins. "Staff" means anyone with chatalyst.command.staff. Console and OP stay above everyone.

yaml
  Hierarchy:
    Enabled: true
    Use_LuckPerms_Weight: true
    Same_Rank_Can_Punish: false

Note

Offline staff members are looked up through LuckPerms. Without LuckPerms the hierarchy only protects them while they are online.

#X-ray

Two tools to catch players who use an x-ray mod or texture pack.

Vision (/xray, the spyglass in the staff kit, or the button in /staff): glowing outlines, seen through stone, on the ores you pick around you. Diamonds, ancient debris, emeralds, any other ore, spawners and chests. Only you see them, and nothing changes in the world. The menu picks the ores and the radius (8 to 48 blocks). It comes with the staff suite; permission chatalyst.command.xray.

Detector (off by default): it watches what players mine and ranks them, so you know whom to watch.

  • It counts veins, not blocks: a vein of 8 diamonds is one find.
  • A blind vein is one whose first block touched no air, water or lava. The player's own tunnel does not count. Nobody can see such an ore without x-ray.
  • It compares valuable veins against the stone mined, forgets old luck over time and ignores ores a player placed and broke again.

Staff with chatalyst.xray.alerts get a message for every valuable vein, with a clickable [TP] to it, and a warning when someone's score gets high. /xray suspects lists suspects by score. The score is a review priority, never proof: always go and watch first.

yaml
Xray:
  Vision:
    Enabled: true
    Default_Radius: 24
    Max_Radius: 48
    Max_Markers: 200
  Detector:
    Enabled: false
    Valuable_Ores: [diamond, debris, emerald]
    Alert_Every_Vein: true
    Alert_Score: 70

#Reasons without typing

The menus offer preset reasons, so staff click instead of type. "Custom reason" is always there too: the staff member types it in chat and it is captured privately. Edit the lists in moderation.yml:

yaml
  Reason_Presets:
    - "Hacks / modified client"
    - "X-Ray"
    - "Toxicity / harassment"
    - "Advertising"
    - "Griefing"
    - "Punishment evasion"

Reason_Presets_es holds the Spanish versions.

#Connections, alts and privacy

/check and /dupeip show accounts that joined from the same connection. Remember that siblings or a shared café also share a connection: it is a clue, not proof.

What is stored. To link accounts, Chatalyst saves a hash of each IP address and the IP address itself. Both stay on your server (or your own database). Nothing is sent anywhere else. This record is kept even while the staff suite is off, so mention it in your privacy policy.

Who sees it. The alt hint in /check needs chatalyst.staff.alts. Real IP addresses are only shown to staff with chatalyst.staff.ip; everyone else sees a short code.

yaml
Staff_Moderation:
  Track_Alts: true
  Alts_Retention_Days: 365
  Import_Old_Connections: true
Option Default What it does
Track_Alts true Groups accounts that share a connection. false stops the grouping, but each player's last connection is still saved so /banip works by name.
Alts_Retention_Days 365 Connection data not seen for this many days is deleted, once a day. 0 = keep it forever. The list of player names stays. With Track_Alts off, the saved last connection (address included) is not deleted.
Import_Old_Connections true On the first start, reads old connections from the server's logs folder and from EssentialsX, so players from before Chatalyst show up too. false = start empty.

Set Import_Old_Connections: false before the first start if you do not want old logs read. Staff with chatalyst.staff.ip can run the import again with /dupeip import.

#Lockdown

/lockdown <reason> stops everyone except staff from joining, on every server that shares the same storage. Use it during a bot attack or when a staff account is stolen. Players who are already online stay.

text
/lockdown Maintenance, back in 10 minutes
/lockdown end

Players with chatalyst.lockdown.bypass can still join.

#Accountability

Every punishment gets a number and goes into the player's history, shown by /check, /history and the history menu.

  • /staffhistory <staff> lists what one staff member did.
  • /staffrollback <staff> <time> is for a stolen account. It lifts the bans that person placed in that time and removes those bans and kicks from player histories. It shows a summary first and waits for your confirmation.
  • /prunehistory <player> [time] deletes old history, also after a confirmation. It does not lift active punishments.

With Staff security on (Staff_Security.Enabled in moderation.yml), sensitive staff commands are also copied to the console and to the private staff Discord channel. See Spy & logs.

Tip

The suite includes more optional guards in moderation.yml: Staff_Item_Audit (items staff spawn), Item_Watch (valuable items in inventories) and Anti_Exploit. Each has its own Enabled switch.
