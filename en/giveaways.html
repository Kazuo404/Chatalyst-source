Optional extras

Giveaways

A live draw the whole server watches, with a spinning roulette of player heads, plus /giveall to give one prize to everyone online.

A giveaway in Chatalyst is a live event. Staff start a draw, everyone gets a countdown, and then a roulette of player heads opens for every eligible player at the same moment. The wheel spins, slows down with rising ticks, and stops on the winner, who gets a title, a broadcast, fireworks and the prize.

When you want no winner at all, /giveall gives one prize to every player online. Staff never win a draw and do not receive /giveall prizes by default, and nobody can be hurt while the wheel is on their screen.

#How to turn it on

Giveaways are an optional extra and ship off, with two example prizes. Set Enabled: true and run /cht reload. Settings live in plugins/Chatalyst/giveaway.yml:

yaml
Giveaway:
  Enabled: false
  Command: "giveaway"
  Command_Aliases:
    - "sorteo"
    - "rifa"
    - "raffle"
  Warmup_Seconds: 20
  Countdown_Marks: [30, 20, 10, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1]
  Ready_Seconds: 3
  Spin_Seconds: 9
  Winner_Seconds: 6
  Reopen_If_Closed: true

Staff open /giveaway, pick a category, then a prize, then start. Or they type /giveaway start <prize id> [seconds] [units or amount] [winners:N], for example:

text
/giveaway start diamonds 30 5 winners:2

That starts the shipped diamond prize after 30 seconds, with 5 units for each of 2 winners.

Every word of the command works in English and in Spanish: start or iniciar, all or todos, cancel or cancelar, list or lista, give or dar.

Note

A server that already had Chatalyst keeps its own giveaway.yml. There the command is still /sorteo, the example prizes keep their old ids and All.Include_Staff keeps the value written in the file. Nothing is changed for you.

#Configuration

#How a draw runs

  1. Warm-up. Everyone is told a draw is about to start, with a chat line, a title and a draining boss bar, and a countdown in the last seconds.
  2. Start. The roulette opens for every eligible player at once and immunity switches on.
  3. Spin. Heads scroll past and slow down; the tick sound rises in pitch.
  4. Winner. Chosen at the very last step, at random among the entrants still online. Then the prize is handed over and immunity fades a few seconds later.
Option Default What it does
Enabled false Turns giveaways on.
Command / Command_Aliases giveaway / sorteo, rifa, raffle Command names. A name another plugin owns is skipped; /chatalyst:giveaway always works.
Warmup_Seconds 20 Time between the announcement and the wheel opening. 0 starts at once.
Countdown_Marks 30, 20, 10, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 Seconds announced during the warm-up.
Ready_Seconds 3 Pause after the menu opens, so everyone can read the prize.
Spin_Seconds 9 How long the wheel spins.
Winner_Seconds 6 How long the winner stays on screen.
Reopen_If_Closed true Reopens the roulette for anyone who closes it during the spin.
Immunity.Enabled true No damage, hunger or burning during the draw.
Immunity.Extra_Seconds 5 Extra protection after the winner is announced.
Immunity.Cancel_Void true Also cancels void damage. Set false if your spawn is over the void.
Firework true A harmless firework at the winner's feet.
Amount_Presets 1000 … 100000 Up to 6 money shortcuts in the menu.
Notify_Discord true Posts the result in the staff Discord channel (needs the Discord bot staff channel).

Sounds sets the sound of each step of the wheel. Each sound can be written short, with only its name, or long, with its volume and pitch:

yaml
Giveaway:
  Sounds:
    Enabled: true
    Start: "BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_BELL"
    Countdown: { Sound: "BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_PLING", Volume: 1.0, Pitch: 1.2 }
Sound Default When it plays
Start BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_BELL When the warm-up starts.
Countdown BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_PLING, pitch 1.2 On each countdown number.
Countdown_Final The Countdown sound, pitch 1.6 On the last three numbers.
Ready The Countdown sound, volume 0.7, pitch 1.4 Each second between the wheel opening and moving.
Tick / Tick_Final BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_HAT, volume 0.6 / BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_PLING Each step of the reel, and each step of the last quarter. Their pitch climbs from Pitch_From (0.7) to Pitch_To (2.0).
Winner ENTITY_PLAYER_LEVELUP, pitch 1.2 The winner fanfare when Show.Reveal.Enabled is false.
Winner_Personal UI_TOAST_CHALLENGE_COMPLETE Only the winner hears it.
Extra_Winner minecraft:block.note_block.chime, pitch 1.2 One per extra winner, a little higher each time.

Sound is a Bukkit name (BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_BELL) or a key (minecraft:block.bell.use). A key from your resource pack works too. Volume goes from 0 to 10 and Pitch from 0.5 to 2.

The texts of those messages are in the language files (giveaway.* and giveaway-show.*). The config only switches them on or off.

A winner in vanish never gets effects that other players can see.

To see the wheel and the reveal without running a draw, type /giveaway preview <prize id>. Only you see it. The winner is made up, nobody is entered and nothing is handed over. Closing the wheel ends the preview.

Participants.Staff_Permissions Chatalyst, vanilla, EssentialsX and LiteBans moderation nodes Any player with one of these counts as staff.

Operators always count as staff. The staff permissions catch a staff rank whatever it is called, even in another language.

#Prizes

Each prize lives under Giveaway_Prizes and has a Type:

Type What it gives
ITEMS Items written in the file (Material, Amount, Name, Lore, Enchantments, Model_Data, Glow).
COMMANDS Console commands. {player} and {uuid} are the winner.
MONEY Money through Vault. The amount is typed when the draw starts, as a figure (50000) or a band (15000-35000) rolled each time.
STORE A product you already sell, by its Store_Catalog id in store.yml. See Store, ranks & Tebex.
RANK A rank. With Tebex enabled for that rank it goes through the same records as a purchase; otherwise it is written to LuckPerms directly.
PERMISSION LuckPerms permission nodes under Permissions, for a perk that is not a whole rank.

Any prize can also carry extra Commands and Permissions. Duration (7d, 30d, 12h or permanent) sets how long the rank and permissions last. This is one of the shipped prizes:

yaml
Giveaway_Prizes:
  diamonds:
    Category: items
    Name: "<green><bold>Diamond stash</bold></green>"
    Name_es: "<green><bold>Alijo de diamantes</bold></green>"
    Type: ITEMS
    Icon: DIAMOND
    Glow: true
    Description:
      - "<gray>32 diamonds and a netherite ingot"
    Description_es:
      - "<gray>32 diamantes y un lingote de netherita"
    Items:
      - Material: DIAMOND
        Amount: 32
      - Material: NETHERITE_INGOT
        Amount: 1

The prize id (diamonds) is what you type in /giveaway start <id>. Keep it short, lowercase and permanent. Category is the menu category the prize is filed under.

A prize's permission list can also be edited in game: /giveaway, right-click the prize, add with the book and shift-click to remove. Saving writes giveaway.yml and keeps its comments.

#Units

You can choose how many units of a prize to give when you launch it, from 1 to 64. This works for two kinds of prize:

  • ITEMS prizes: every stack is multiplied.
  • COMMANDS prizes that have {amount} in a command. {amount} in Name or Description shows the chosen number.
yaml
Commands:
  - "crates give {player} legendary {amount}"

In the menu, use the Amount button on the launch screen. In a command, write the number in the place shown in the tables below. A COMMANDS prize without {amount} takes no units.

#Prizes made in game

You can build a prize without editing the file. /giveaway create <id> [name] creates it and opens a chest where you put the items.

Command What it does
/giveaway edit <id> Opens the prize's chest again.
/giveaway name <id> <text> Changes the visible name.
/giveaway description <id> <text> Adds a description line. Use clear as the text to clear them.
/giveaway command <id> add|remove|list [command or number] Edits the console commands ({player}, {uuid}).
/giveaway icon <id> Uses the item in your hand as the icon.
/giveaway winners <id> <n> Default number of winners (1-10).
/giveaway view <id> / /giveaway custom Shows one prize or lists them all.
/giveaway delete <id> confirm Deletes a prize.
/giveaway history [n] Shows recent winners.

All of them need chatalyst.command.giveaway.admin. The Spanish words (crear, editar, nombre, borrar…) work too.

#Automatic giveaways

Schedules starts draws by itself. Each entry runs at most once per day and time. The shipped example is off:

yaml
Giveaway:
  Time_Zone: server
  Schedules:
    weekend-example:
      Enabled: false
      Time: "21:00"
      Days: [SAT, SUN]
      Prize: diamonds
      Winners: 2
      Warmup_Seconds: 60
      Min_Players: 5

Time_Zone: server uses the clock of the machine the server runs on. To pin it, write a zone name such as Europe/Madrid, America/New_York or UTC. A file that already names a zone keeps it.

Days are written MON, TUE, WED, THU, FRI, SAT, SUN. Full names (MONDAY, SATURDAY) and the Spanish ones (LUN, MIE, SABADO) work too. An empty list means every day. In a network, set a schedule on one server only, or each server starts its own.

#Ranks as prizes

With Ranks.Auto_Detect: true, groups named in Ranks.Names (vip, mvp++, legend…) that exist in LuckPerms are offered as prizes automatically. Groups in Ranks.Never and any group that grants a staff permission are never offered. Ranks.Duration (default permanent) is how long a directly granted rank lasts.

/giveaway ranks lists the ranks on offer. /giveaway rank <group> <duration|permanent|on|off|config> changes one: a duration such as 30d, on or off to offer it or not, or config to go back to the file value.

#The menus

Giveaway_Categories groups prizes in the menu (kits, ranks, money, perks, items and custom prizes). Each prize names its category with Category. Two category ids have a job: ranks found in LuckPerms go under ranks, and prizes made in game go under custom. Cards are centered automatically, keep the order of the file, and go to more pages (previous / next) when they do not fit.

The status button shows what is happening (nothing, countdown with its seconds, handing out, wheel spinning) and updates while the menu is open. Click it to cancel, if you have the permission.

#Confirmation screen

Before a draw or a prize for everyone starts, a summary shows the prize with its icon, how many units or how much money, how many players receive it right now, who is left out and why, the countdown and the animation. It has three buttons:

  • Launch: starts it. It is greyed out, with the reason, when nobody would receive it, another giveaway is running or the prize hands out nothing.
  • Preview: plays the animation or the wheel for you only, then brings you back. Nothing is given.
  • Back: returns to the previous screen.

Set Giveaway_Menu.Confirm: false to launch at once.

#A prize for everyone

/giveall (also /darall and /keyall) gives one prize to every player online. Other servers call this a "keyall". There is no menu for players and no wheel: a countdown runs, then the prize arrives in front of each player, in random order, and is handed over. Players keep moving the whole time. How the prize arrives is up to you: see Animations.

It needs giveaways turned on and Giveaway.All.Enabled, which ships as true.

#What you can give

You type What everyone gets
/giveall Nothing yet. It opens the catalogue so you can pick a prize. Players only.
/giveall <prize id> [units or amount] [seconds] A prize from your catalogue.
/giveall hand [amount] [seconds] Copies of the item in your main hand.
/giveall money <amount> [seconds] That much money each.
/giveall cancel Stops the one that is counting down.
/giveall preview <prize id or hand> [style] Nothing. It plays the animation for you only. See Preview.
text
/giveall diamonds 3 15
/giveall hand 2
/giveall money 5000 30
  • The first line gives 3 units of the diamond prize after a 15 second countdown. The units come before the seconds. For a money prize from the catalogue, write the money amount in the units place.
  • The second line gives every player 2 copies of the item you are holding, with its name, enchantments and lore. You keep yours. With no number, each player gets as many as you hold. The most is 64 per player.
  • The third line pays every player 5000 after 30 seconds. You can also write a band, such as 1000-5000, and each player gets a different amount inside it. It needs Vault and an economy plugin.

Leave the seconds out and the countdown lasts All.Warmup_Seconds. Write 0 to give it at once.

The Spanish words mano, dinero, cancelar and vista work too. If one of your prizes is called hand or money, the prize wins: your catalogue comes before these words. /giveaway all does the same as /giveall.

/giveall cancel works during the countdown. Once the handout starts it lasts a few seconds and cannot be stopped, so nobody is left out halfway.

In the /giveaway menu, the button at the top switches between one winner by wheel and giving to everyone.

Warning

/giveall hand creates items: every player gets a copy of what you hold. Give chatalyst.command.giveall.hand only to people you trust with that.

#Who receives it

Operators do not receive it out of the box. A prize for everyone is a prize for your players. This includes the operator who launches it. Moderators and helpers receive it like any other player.

When you launch one, the message tells you how many players receive it and how many staff were left out, so it never looks like a mistake.

Who counts as staff:

  • Operators.
  • Anyone with the permission chatalyst.giveall.staff. Give it to a rank you also want left out.
  • With All.Staff_Includes_Moderators: true, also anyone the Participants.Staff_Permissions and Participants.Staff_Groups lists recognise (see who takes part). Use it if moderators and helpers should not receive prizes either.

To let staff receive prizes like everybody else, change one line in giveaway.yml and run /cht reload:

yaml
Giveaway:
  All:
    Include_Staff: true

For a bot or a test account that must never receive anything, give it chatalyst.giveall.exempt. It is left out whatever Include_Staff says.

Whoever joins during the countdown also receives it. Vanished players receive it with no visible effect. Excluded_Worlds and Countdown_Marks apply here too. Win_Cooldown and chatalyst.giveaway.exempt do not: receiving this is not winning a draw.

#Animations

Giveaway.All.Animation decides how a prize for everyone arrives, what it looks like and how it sounds. It never changes who receives the prize or when. Out of the box it does what /giveall always did: the prize spins down in front of each player.

#Styles

Choose one with Animation.Style:

Style What the player sees
SPIN_DOWN The prize spins down in front of their face and slows as it lands. This is the default.
RAIN Several small copies fall around them, one after another. The last one falls in front of their face and is the one they get.
BURST The prize appears above them, grows, swells and bursts into particles.
ORBIT The prize circles them in a closing spiral, lower on each turn, until it reaches them.
NONE No object and no particles. Only the sounds and the messages.

With SPIN_DOWN and BURST the prize stays in front of the player's face as they look around. With RAIN and ORBIT it stays where it started, so the player can turn and watch it.

#Options

yaml
Giveaway:
  All:
    Animation:
      Style: SPIN_DOWN
      Duration_Ticks: 26
      Height: 2.5
      Distance: 1.1
      Scale_From: 0.2

      Spin_Speed: 27.7

A few notes on how to write the values:

  • Sounds are written short (Start: "BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_BELL") or long (Start: { Sound: "BLOCK_NOTE_BLOCK_BELL", Volume: 1.0, Pitch: 1.0 }). Volume goes from 0 to 10 and Pitch from 0.5 to 2.
  • Colors are a name (WHITE, RED, GOLD, AQUA, LIME, PURPLE, ORANGE, PINK…) or "#RRGGBB". Keep the quotes: without them YAML reads the # as a comment.
  • Boss bar colors are PINK, BLUE, RED, GREEN, YELLOW, PURPLE or WHITE. Styles are PROGRESS, NOTCHED_6, NOTCHED_10, NOTCHED_12 or NOTCHED_20.
  • The texts of the messages are in the language files (giveaway-all.*), in both languages. The config only switches them on or off.

A mistyped value does not break anything. If a particle, sound, color or style does not exist, the console says so once, with the exact path of the option, and the default is used:

text
giveaway.yml: Giveaway.All.Animation.Particles.Type 'SPARKLES' is not a known particle; using DUST

Note

A giveaway.yml from before 1.0.1 has Sounds and Fireworks directly under All. They keep working until you change the ones under Animation.

#Per-prize animation

Any prize in Giveaway_Prizes can carry its own Animation section, with the same keys. Write only what changes. Everything else comes from Giveaway.All.Animation.

This key circles the player with a golden outline before it reaches them:

yaml
Giveaway_Prizes:
  keys:
    Name: "<white>5 legendary keys"
    Type: COMMANDS
    Icon: TRIPWIRE_HOOK
    Commands:
      - "crates give {player} legendary 5"
    Animation:
      Style: ORBIT
      Duration_Ticks: 50
      Orbit_Turns: 3
      Glow: true
      Glow_Color: GOLD
      Particles:
        Color: GOLD
        Second_Color: YELLOW
      Sounds:
        Receive: { Sound: "BLOCK_ENDER_CHEST_OPEN", Volume: 1.0, Pitch: 1.4 }
      Fireworks:
        Colors: [GOLD, YELLOW]
        Type: STAR

When the same prize is drawn on the wheel, three parts of its Animation change the reveal: Fireworks (the rockets around the winner), Particles (the spiral) and Sounds.Receive (the sound only the winner hears). The rest of the section has no effect on the wheel.

Prizes made in game, /giveall hand and /giveall money have no entry in the file, so they use the general animation.

#Preview

/giveall preview <prize id or hand> [style] plays the whole animation for you only. Nothing is handed over, nobody else sees or hears it, the server is not told and there is no countdown.

text
/giveall preview keys
/giveall preview diamonds rain
/giveall preview hand orbit
  • The first line shows the keys prize with its own animation.
  • The second shows the diamond prize with the RAIN style, just this once. Use it to compare styles without editing the file.
  • The third shows the item in your hand arriving with ORBIT.

It needs chatalyst.command.giveall.preview. The Spanish words previa and vista work too. After you edit giveaway.yml, run /cht reload and preview again.

For the wheel, /giveaway preview <prize id> shows the roulette and the reveal to you only. See the wheel show.

#Streamer giveaway

A streamer raffles their own items: a stack of crate keys somebody gave them, a sword, a full shulker box. Nothing is created and nothing comes from the catalogue. The items leave the streamer's inventory, wait in escrow and go to the winners.

#Turn it on

It ships off. Giveaways must be on too. Set this in giveaway.yml, run /cht reload, and give chatalyst.giveaway.streamer to your streamer rank:

yaml
Giveaway:
  Enabled: true
  Streamer:
    Enabled: true

#How it works

  1. The streamer types /streamgiveaway (also /sgiveaway, /sorteostream). A chest opens.
  2. A click on an item in their inventory moves the whole stack into the chest. Shift-click on an item in the chest takes it back.
  3. At the bottom they choose the number of winners, the sign-up time and how players enter, then press Launch. A summary shows the stacks, the winners and how many players are online to take part. Confirm and launch starts it.
  4. The whole server is told, with every item listed. Hover an item to see its real tooltip. A bar counts down.
  5. When the time is up, the roulette spins once per winner. Stacks are dealt to the winners in turns, in the order they were put in. There are never more winners than stacks or participants.

With Entry: JOIN, players press the button in the chat or type /streamgiveaway join. With Entry: ALL, everyone online with chatalyst.giveaway.join takes part. The streamer never takes part in their own giveaway, and vanished players are not drawn.

Only one giveaway runs at a time: a streamer giveaway, a wheel and a /giveall wait for each other.

#Commands and permissions of streamer giveaways

Command Who What it does
/streamgiveaway chatalyst.giveaway.streamer (nobody by default) Opens the chest.
/streamgiveaway join chatalyst.giveaway.join (everybody) Takes part.
/streamgiveaway claim everybody Collects the items waiting for you.
/streamgiveaway cancel the streamer, or chatalyst.giveaway.admin (op) Cancels the running one. Every item not yet won goes back. Works from the console.

#Streamer options

All under Giveaway.Streamer:

Option Default What it does
Enabled false Turns streamer giveaways on.
Command / Command_Aliases streamgiveaway / sgiveaway, sorteostream Command names. A name another plugin owns is skipped.
Max_Stacks 27 Stacks that fit in the chest (1-45).
Max_Winners 5 Most winners the streamer can pick.
Durations_Seconds 30, 60, 120, 300 Sign-up times to choose from. The first is preselected.
Entry JOIN JOIN or ALL. The streamer can switch it in the chest.
Min_Participants 2 With fewer, it is cancelled and everything goes back.
Cooldown_Seconds 300 Wait between two launches of the same streamer.
Blocked_Materials bedrock, barrier, command blocks, structure blocks, jigsaw, debug stick, light Items that cannot be raffled. Shulker boxes with contents are allowed.

The buttons of the chest are changed under Giveaway_Menu: Streamer_Screen and Streamer_Confirm_Screen set their column (1-9) in the bottom row, and Items.Stream_* their material and Model_Data.

#When something goes wrong

The items are saved to disk before they leave the inventory, so each stack is always in exactly one place.

  • Closing the chest without launching, Cancel, dying or leaving with the chest open: everything goes back.
  • The streamer leaves during a giveaway: it is cancelled and everything not yet won goes back to them.
  • /cht reload, a restart or a crash: whatever had not been won goes back to the streamer, and what was won stays with the winner.
  • A player on the death screen gets their items after respawning.
  • A full inventory, or a player who is offline: the items wait, with no time limit. The player is told on joining and collects them with /streamgiveaway claim.
  • Turning the feature off keeps /streamgiveaway claim working while something is waiting.

Each streamer giveaway leaves lines in the console: who started it, which items, who got what and what was returned.

Note

Items wait on the server where they were raffled. In a network, collect them there.

#Commands and permissions

Command Permission (default) What it does
/giveaway (/sorteo, /rifa, /raffle) chatalyst.command.giveaway (true) Players see the status or reopen a running wheel. Staff open the menu.
/giveaway start <id> [seconds] [units or amount] [winners:N] chatalyst.command.giveaway.admin (op) Starts a draw. The fourth value is the money amount for money prizes, otherwise the units.
/giveaway cancel chatalyst.command.giveaway.admin Cancels the running draw or the countdown for everyone.
/giveaway list chatalyst.command.giveaway.admin Lists prizes and any problem with them.
/giveaway give <player> <id> [units or amount] chatalyst.command.giveaway.admin Hands a prize over by hand.
/giveaway status chatalyst.command.giveaway.admin Shows the running draw or the last winner.
/giveaway preview <id> chatalyst.command.giveaway.admin Shows the wheel and the reveal to you only, with a made-up winner.
/giveall and /giveall <id> [units or amount] [seconds] (/darall, /keyall) chatalyst.command.giveall (op) Opens the give-to-everyone menu, or gives a catalogue prize to everyone.
/giveall hand [amount] [seconds] chatalyst.command.giveall.hand (op) Gives everyone copies of the item in your hand.
/giveall money <amount> [seconds] chatalyst.command.giveall.money (op) Gives everyone that much money.
/giveall cancel chatalyst.command.giveall.cancel (op) Stops a prize for everyone during its countdown.
/giveall preview <id or hand> [style] chatalyst.command.giveall.preview (op) Plays the animation for you only. Nothing is handed over.

Three more permissions decide who receives and who wins:

Permission Default What it does
chatalyst.giveall.staff op Counts as staff for /giveall. Left out unless All.Include_Staff is true.
chatalyst.giveall.exempt false Never receives a /giveall prize, staff or not. For bots and test accounts.
chatalyst.giveaway.exempt false Never drawn as the winner of a wheel.

chatalyst.command.giveaway.admin includes the five /giveall permissions and chatalyst.giveall.staff. Whoever already ran giveaways keeps everything.

The /giveall permissions let you hand out less than the whole thing. A moderator with only chatalyst.command.giveall can give catalogue prizes to everyone, but cannot start a wheel, edit prizes, give away the item in their hand or give money. Add .hand, .money, .cancel and .preview on top of it, one by one, as you see fit. They are extras: give them together with chatalyst.command.giveall, which is what makes the command itself available.

#Limits

  • A winner who is on the death screen gets the prize when they respawn.
  • A winner who disconnects gets the prize when they return. If the server restarts first, the console shows the /giveaway give command staff need to run.
  • Items that do not fit in the winner's inventory drop at their feet.
  • RANK and PERMISSION prizes need LuckPerms. MONEY and /giveall money need Vault.
  • A /giveall hand or /giveall money prize is not saved in the catalogue. If a player disconnects before receiving it, they get it when they return, but only until the next restart: the console says so and staff hand it over by hand.
  • /giveall hand gives at most 64 of the item to each player. Only a player can use it: the console has no hand.
  • /giveall cancel cannot stop a handout that has already started.
  • Bedrock players do not see the arriving prize, whatever the style. They get the particles, the sounds and the messages.
  • A preview of the wheel leaves out the totem animation, which cannot be shown to one player alone. A real draw that starts while you watch a preview replaces it.
  • You can watch one preview at a time. If the server is already at Max_Entities, /giveall preview asks you to try again in a few seconds.
