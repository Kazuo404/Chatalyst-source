Moderation

Anonymous mode

An incognito mode for streamers and staff - a believable fake name and skin, with rank, tags and party hidden everywhere.

Anonymous mode lets a player play without being recognized. It is made for streamers and for staff who want to watch the server as an ordinary player. With /anonymous, the player gets a believable fake name and skin, and their rank, tags and party disappear from chat, the tab list, nametags and hovers.

Staff can always find out who is behind an alias with /realname <alias>.

#Turn it on

Anonymous mode ships off. In players.yml:

yaml
Anonymous:
  Enabled: true

Run /cht reload. Players need chatalyst.command.anonymous (operators have it) and type /anonymous to switch it on or off.

#How the disguise looks

yaml
  Name_Style: "accounts"
  Rotate_Name_On_Join: true
  Simulate_Reconnect: true
Option What it does
Name_Style accounts uses the real name and skin of an account from a list, so name and face match. realistic makes up names like ShadowWolf42. confusing makes unreadable names like illiliolil.
Rotate_Name_On_Join A new alias and skin on every connection, so nobody can follow the player across days.
Simulate_Reconnect When switching on, others see the player "leave" and a new player "join" a little later, instead of a name changing in place.

An alias never matches anyone who has played on your server.

#What gets hidden

  • Rank, /tag and party, in every chat format, the tab list, nametags, hovers and clicks.
  • Money and time played in the chat hover.
  • The real name in messages from other plugins, such as death messages, titles and action bars.

Note

The name above the head is hidden by the fancy nametag or by TAB. Keep one of them on for full anonymity.

#Records for staff

When a player enters anonymous mode, the chat log keeps the real name and the alias (Log.Anonymous in moderation.yml). It is the only record of who is behind a disguise, so staff can investigate later without unmasking anyone live. Set it to false to leave no trace.
