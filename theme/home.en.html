How the chat works

  1. 01

    A format for every rank →

    One file, formats/chat.yml, decides how each rank's messages look: prefix, name, colours and what comes after.

  2. 02

    Hover a name, know the player →

    Every name opens a card with rank, party, money, playtime and ping. Click it to send a private message.

  3. 03

    Talk where it makes sense →

    Global, staff and local channels. A short prefix sends one message to another channel without switching.

  4. 04

    A panel for your staff →

    Off by default. Turn it on and your team follows chat, reports and players from a browser.
